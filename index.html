A few ground rules

Make stuff.
Be good humans.

Keep it fair, collaborative, and welcoming. We will share the final event handbook with registered teams before the event.

  1. Build during the eventStart a new project when the hackathon begins. You may use existing open-source libraries, APIs, and tools—credit them clearly.
  2. Teams of 2–4 studentsAll team members must be currently enrolled college students. Each person may join one team only.
  3. Make it yoursYour submission must be your team's original work. Do not copy another team's project or submit work you do not have permission to use.
  4. Submit on timeShare a working demo, source-code link, and short project description before the announced deadline.
  5. Be respectful and safeFollow the event code of conduct, respect other participants, and use data and AI responsibly.
  6. Show the impactJudges will look at usefulness, originality, technical execution, and the clarity of your presentation.
